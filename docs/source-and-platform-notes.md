## Platform Notes

### Instagram

Use strong visual assets. First line should carry the offer or hook. Keep hashtags focused and local.

### Facebook

Use slightly fuller context. Mention Mox Boarding House Chandler and the beginner-friendly table. Local groups require rule checks before posting.

### TikTok

Use simple scripts, direct hooks, and clear captions. Still-image posts can work if paired with music, but do not rely on captions alone.

### Threads, Bluesky, X

Short hooks, direct links, and one clear CTA. Avoid hashtag piles.

### LinkedIn

Use process posts: UX writing, learning design, facilitation, onboarding, and building local creative experiences. Do not make direct ticket-sale asks.

### Reddit

Community-first. Read rules before posting. Ask permission when in doubt. Avoid repeating the same promo in multiple local communities.
